    How the value is built

    —

    TVL is the protocol's locked value in the snapshot. Growth is its seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150%. Maturity is logarithmic in days since the snapshot first tracked the protocol, so three years of running scores near the top without crushing a young protocol to zero. Breadth rewards running on more chains, at most +20%. The watchlist — every protocol at or above $50,000,000 of TVL that records no token — is the floor, never the ceiling: the board also ranks every other pre-token protocol the snapshot carries.
